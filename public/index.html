            Rules and privacy

            Who can use Blip

            You must be 18 or older. Blip isn’t designed for children.

            What isn’t allowed

            Harassment, threats, scams, spam, sexual or explicit content, anything involving the exploitation of minors, illegal content, and sharing other people’s private information. The people who run Blip may block anyone who breaks these rules.

            What Blip keeps

            Messages, photos, PDFs and voice notes are held in the server’s memory while the chat runs and erased when it ends, including if the server restarts. Blip has no accounts, ads or tracking cookies. Your network address is used briefly to limit abuse, and your browser keeps a small note for this tab so you can rejoin if your connection drops.

            If you report something

            A report sends the names in the chat, the reason you chose and the last 20 text messages to the people who run Blip. Files are never included.

            Know the limits

            Anonymous isn’t untraceable, and other people can screenshot or save what you share. Photos have location data removed before they’re sent, but PDFs and voice notes are shared as they are. Links in messages aren’t clickable, and photos from others start hidden.

            Emergencies

            Blip is not for emergencies. Call your local emergency number. If you see child sexual abuse material, report it to the authorities in your country, such as the NCMEC CyberTipline in the US.

            No guarantees

            Blip is provided as is, without promises about uptime or about who you’ll meet. You use it at your own risk.
